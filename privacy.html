3. This website

mariogruber.com sets no cookies and has no forms, advertising or tracking tools. To count visits, it uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which records the pages viewed, the site you came from, your country and your type of device and browser, without cookies or other storage on your device and without identifying you. We look at these figures only in summary form, in our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used. If you email or call us from the site, we use your details as set out in section 2. Our hosting provider keeps short-lived server logs, including IP addresses, to run and secure the site. The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address to Google when the page opens.

4. Where we get it

From you, or from public sources such as Companies House, published council documents, organisations' websites and professional networking profiles.

When we work for a client, for example on stakeholder engagement or a public consultation, the client decides how personal data is used and its privacy notice applies. We act only on the client's instructions.

Only where needed: our IT, hosting, email, cloud storage and AI service providers, under data processing terms (AI tools we use do not train on your data); contractors working for us under confidentiality; our accountant and professional advisers; and public authorities where the law requires. We never sell personal data.

7. Transfers outside the UK

Some of our service providers and contractors are based outside the UK. We transfer personal data only to countries covered by UK adequacy regulations, or under the International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. How long we keep it

Business contacts: while the relationship is active, reviewed every two years. Contracts, invoices and financial records: six years after the end of the financial year. Complaints and rights requests: six years. We then delete the data securely.

9. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct or delete it, to restrict or stop using it, or to transfer it to you or another organisation. You can object to our use of it at any time, and we always stop marketing when you ask. We reply within one month, free of charge. We make no decisions about you by automated means alone.
